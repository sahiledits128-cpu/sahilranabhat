About
Sahil Ranabhat · Age 19 · Chitwan, Nepal

The designer behind the work.

Sahil Ranabhat is a 19-year-old designer from Chitwan, Nepal, specializing in thumbnail design, branding and social media design. He's already worked with 1000+ clients and helped generate 1.5M+ views across TikTok and YouTube — turning ideas into visually strong, scroll-stopping content.

What started as late nights tinkering with type and color has grown into a full creative practice: helping brands and creators show up online with visuals that feel considered, not generic — from a single Instagram post to a full brand campaign.
